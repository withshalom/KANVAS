Grid size

A fixed spacing for this sheet \u2014 exact at any zoom, the way real graph paper does not change size when you lean in. It shows on screen only: grid lines never appear in an exported file.

Custom size

New sheet

One entry per trim. Every sheet turns with the Portrait / Landscape control or the Turn buttons, so there is no separate landscape preset to hunt for.

Keyboard

Single keys pick tools. Modifiers act on the document. On touch, two fingers that move transform the view — spread to zoom, slide to pan, twist to turn the sheet. Two fingers that stay put place a shape between them, whenever the active tool takes two points.
