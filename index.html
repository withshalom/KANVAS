Keyboard

Every tool, input and brush is a single bare letter — no modifier is ever required. Ctrl/Cmd acts on the document. On touch, two fingers that move transform the view — spread to zoom, slide to pan, twist to turn the sheet. Two fingers that stay put place a shape between them, whenever the active tool takes two points.
